
# E-Commerce Sales Analysis & Business Intelligence
## Analyse E-Commerce Sales to Improve Customer Conversion & Retention

---

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce data to understand customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, profitability, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow using Python, SQL, Excel, Statistics, and Power BI to generate data-driven business insights and recommendations.

---

## Business Objectives

1. Analyze product sales and purchasing behaviour across categories, brands, and customer segments.
2. Understand customer order behaviour, retention patterns, delivery performance, and regional trends.
3. Evaluate pricing, discounts, shipping costs, revenue, and profitability.
4. Analyze customer reviews and ratings to understand satisfaction and product performance.

---

## Dataset

The project uses six interconnected datasets:

- **Customers** – Customer demographics and segments
- **Orders** – Order status, payment, and order/delivery dates
- **Order Items** – Quantity, unit price, discount, and shipping cost
- **Products** – Categories, subcategories, brands, prices, and stock
- **Order Reviews** – Customer ratings and feedback
- **Geolocation** – City, state, region, and location details

## Analysis

### Python & EDA

Python was used for data cleaning, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, visualization, and business insights.

### SQL

SQL analysis covers:

- Sales performance
- Customer behaviour
- Product and category analysis
- Profitability
- Discounts and shipping
- Reviews and delivery performance

### Excel

Excel is used for KPI analysis, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and supporting business analysis.

### Statistical Analysis

Statistical techniques such as descriptive statistics, correlation, t-test, ANOVA, and Chi-square will be applied where appropriate.

### Power BI

An interactive dashboard will be developed to analyze:

- Sales & Product Performance
- Customer Behaviour
- Profitability
- Delivery Performance
- Customer Satisfaction

### Machine Learning

Machine Learning will be implemented only where the available data supports a meaningful business prediction problem.
